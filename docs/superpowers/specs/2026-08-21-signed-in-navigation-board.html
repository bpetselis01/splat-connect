Navigation architecture · option B

One header that never moves.

SPLAT Connect is 48 public pages and 15 account pages. Today, signing in replaces the whole navigation model and hides three quarters of the product. Approach B keeps the header a parent learned while signed out — in the same place, forever — and makes the dashboard the eighth section rather than a different world.

Header recedes · rail and content own the page

What changed Height 44 → 36px · pills lose the 0 2px 0 hard shadow · tone dots go neutral grey · label colour brand-deep → muted · background surface → a near-white tint. Nothing is removed, resized past legibility, or hover-gated.

What this costs, honestly

The dark rail you built goes away as a navigation device. That is the real price of B, and it is worth naming rather than burying — components/rail.tsx, components/shell-frame.tsx and lib/nav-model.ts would be replaced by an eighth entry in lib/public-nav.ts plus a subnav strip. In exchange you delete a whole parallel nav system, and PUBLIC_NAV becomes the single source of truth it already claims to be in its own docstring.
