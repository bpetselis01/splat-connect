# SPLAT Connect

SPLAT (Supporting Play by Adapting Toys) is a platform where families, makers and organisations share the knowledge and the physical things that make play accessible: adapted toys, how-to guides, and the people who build and print them.

## Language

### Content

**Guide**:
Step-by-step instructions for adapting a toy or making an assistive device. Reviewed before it is public.
_Avoid_: Tutorial, lesson, course

**Guide library**:
The public, searchable collection of approved Guides.
_Avoid_: Library (bare), tutorials

**Toy library**:
The public collection of Toys offered by families and Organisations.
_Avoid_: Library (bare), listings

**Idea**:
Any submission to the Challenge board, before the team decides what to do with it.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, request

**Challenge**:
An Idea promoted to the public board so makers can take it on.
_Avoid_: Brief, problem

**Question**:
An Idea that asks the community for help instead of proposing a build. It is resolved by an answer the asker marks, never by a Guide.
_Avoid_: Help request, query

**Toy**:
A physical adapted toy a Member or an Organisation owns and offers, with photos and a grade.
_Avoid_: Listing, item

**Given away**:
A Toy you no longer have, because a Gift or Swap of it completed. Worked out from Exchanges, never stored on the Toy.
_Avoid_: Archived, lent

### Exchanges

**Exchange**:
Anything that passes between two parties on the platform, with its own thread and handover. Comes in four kinds: Gift, Swap, Build and Print.
_Avoid_: Transaction (code-only name), trade

**Gift**:
An Exchange where a Toy is given for nothing.
_Avoid_: Donation (code value only)

**Swap**:
An Exchange where a Toy is traded for another.
_Avoid_: Exchange (for this kind alone)

**Build**:
An Exchange where a family asks a maker to make a Toy from a Guide. Exactly one Maker claims it; there are no shared builds.

**Print**:
An Exchange where a family asks a printer's owner to print files for a Guide.

**Step**:
Where an Exchange is in its life, such as asked, accepted, printing, ready, closed. Worked out from its status and timestamps.
_Avoid_: Stage

**Editor step**:
One page of the Toy editor wizard (details, photos, review). Unrelated to an Exchange.
_Avoid_: Step (bare), stage

### People and trust

**Member**:
Any signed-in account. Everyone who is not an Admin is a Member, whether they signed up as a Family or a Maker.
_Avoid_: Contributor, parent, user

**Family**:
What a Member says they are at signup when they want accessible play for a child. A signup choice, not a role.
_Avoid_: Parent

**Maker**:
What a Member says they are at signup when they want to build, print or write Guides. A signup choice, not a role.
_Avoid_: Contributor

**Child profile**:
A Member's record of one child's answers to the four child questions, used to recommend Guides and Toys.
_Avoid_: Ability profile

**Leader**:
A Member an Admin has made a leader of an Organisation. A Member may lead several.
_Avoid_: Organiser, manager

**Admin**:
A Member with the admin role. Reviews Guides and Ideas and grants Leadership and Verified.
_Avoid_: Moderator

**Author**:
The Member who created a Guide and owns it. Every Guide has one.
_Avoid_: Primary contributor, owner

**Collaborator**:
A Member the Author has invited to co-edit one Guide.
_Avoid_: Co-author, contributor

**Organisation**:
A group that runs on the platform, such as a school, charity or maker space, with its own leaders and public page.
_Avoid_: Organization, org (in UI and prose)

**Verified**:
A trust mark that only an admin grants, and only to an Organisation or a Printer. A Printer earns it with a checked test print and three completed Print jobs. A person is never Verified.
_Avoid_: Approved, trusted, certified
